# Background: Exide and the Gateway Cities

Fact-checked context for this project. Every fact has a source and the date it was true.

**Status key**
- **Verified:** stated by a primary source (agency, court, legislature)
- **Reported:** stated by reputable news coverage
- **Claim:** a party's position, not established fact
- **Open:** not yet confirmed

## Huntington Park High School (PIA school PSCH-05)

| Fact | Status | As of | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| DTSC sampled the campus as PIA school PSCH-05; lead in the grass quad measured 100–110 ppm. | Reported | 2016-08 | [LAist](https://laist.com/news/elevated-levels-of-lead-found-in-fi) |
| DTSC's letter of 2016-05-02 states no screening level exists for high school students, so results were compared to the 80 ppm residential level (OEHHA, adopted by DTSC in 2009). That level is modeled with LeadSpread for children aged 0–7 exposed at home 350 days/year, 24 hours/day; at 80 ppm it predicts a 1 µg/dL blood lead increase at the 90th percentile, about a 1-point IQ decrease. | Verified | 2016-05-02 | [DTSC letter to LAUSD, PSCH-05 report](https://www.envirostor.dtsc.ca.gov/getfile?filename=/public%2Fdeliverable_documents%2F8014738321%2FPSCH-05%20%20Huntington%20Park%20HS%20Rpt.pdf) |
| The same letter adjusts exposure from 7 to 5 days/week (a school week), raising the equivalent level to 110 ppm for a young child, and concludes high school students are less susceptible and have less soil contact. HPHS results (100–110 ppm) fall at or below that adjusted level. | Verified | 2016-05-02 | [DTSC letter to LAUSD, PSCH-05 report](https://www.envirostor.dtsc.ca.gov/getfile?filename=/public%2Fdeliverable_documents%2F8014738321%2FPSCH-05%20%20Huntington%20Park%20HS%20Rpt.pdf) |
| Athletic fields were not sampled. | Reported | 2016-08 | [LAist](https://laist.com/news/elevated-levels-of-lead-found-in-fi) (quoting a USC professor) |
| LAUSD prepared a soil Removal Action Workplan for the campus (draft final 2017-05-03). Contaminant, reason, and any link to Exide or campus construction unknown. Original link returned 404 on 2026-10-15. | Open | 2017-05 | [LAUSD RAW](https://www.lausd.org/cms/lib/CA01000043/Centricity/Domain/135/Huntington%20Park%20HS%20RAW%20DRAFT%20FINAL_03MAY2017.pdf) |
| The campus sampling for PSCH-13 (San Antonio Continuation, apparently adjacent to the HPHS field per aerial image) placed all five samples near buildings and planters; the HPHS football field, baseball field and track were not sampled. | Verified | 2016 | Parsons sample location map, Figure 2, PSCH-13 report |
| Critique: DTSC's no-cleanup reasoning assumed limited soil contact by high school students. Daily PE, soccer, and field use suggest higher contact than assumed. | Open (critique) | 2016 | Unsampled fields: LAist, quoting Julie Johnston, USC environmental health professor. Field use: local knowledge. |
